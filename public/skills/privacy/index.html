Privacy: skills for AI assistants

Updated November 11, 2026

Applies to the skills for AI assistants that Herman Harazha publishes on GitHub: convert.

The skills collect and store no personal data. They have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry.

A skill is a text file of instructions and small scripts. It runs inside the assistant a person chose, on the files that person names. The scripts send nothing over the network.

Original files are not changed. Results are saved where the person said.

What happens to the conversation itself is governed by the privacy policy of the company that runs the assistant.

Questions: geragaraza@gmail.com or the Issues of the skill's repository.
