Privacy: Merum for iPhone and Mac

Updated November 25, 2026

Applies to Merum by Herman Harazha: the iPhone app in the App Store and the Mac app from GitHub.

Merum collects no personal data and sends nothing to its developer. The developer has no servers; Merum has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Notes are kept on the device, in the app's own folder, until the person deletes them. On the iPhone they are part of the iPhone's backup, like any app's documents.

Notes move between the person's own devices through iCloud, in the private database of that person's Apple Account; the sort order and text size go through iCloud key-value storage. The developer cannot see either. Without iCloud everything stays on the device.

A picture pasted into a note is read on the device by Apple's Vision and only its text is kept; a pasted file becomes its path. Neither is sent anywhere.

Besides iCloud, the network is used only by the Mac app, to check GitHub for updates once a day.

A note leaves the app only where the person sends it, through Share, Save, or the clipboard.
